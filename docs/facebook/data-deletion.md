# User Data Deletion Instructions

Last Updated: February 2026

If you want your data deleted from our system, you may request deletion at any time.

## How to Request Data Deletion

Please send an email to:

kbgarcia8@gmail.com

Include the following information:

- The email address associated with your account
- A request to delete your account and associated data

## What Happens Next

Once your request is received:

1. We will verify the account.
2. Your account and associated data will be permanently deleted.
3. You will receive confirmation once the deletion is completed.

Data deletion requests are typically processed within 48 hours.

If you logged in using Facebook Login, removing the application from your Facebook account will also prevent future access to your data.
